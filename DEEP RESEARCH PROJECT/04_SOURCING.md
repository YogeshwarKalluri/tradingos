## The three things that actually determine profit per truck

**1. Distance.** Freight is $31–$45 per unit on a 480-mile load. On a 200-mile Ohio load it is
$13–$21. **That $20–$25 per unit is 16–20% of your entire contribution margin, and it is a
permanent, structural, un-competed advantage** available to a Michigan operator and to almost
nobody bidding against you on a Pennsylvania load. Whirlpool is headquartered in Benton
Harbor, Michigan with plants across Ohio; GE is in Louisville. **Your sourcing map should be
drawn as concentric circles around Detroit, and Shippensburg PA is outside the useful one.**

**2. Whether you are bidding or buying.** Auctions transfer the entire surplus to the seller.
The observed Lowe's clearing price of $188–$245/unit is above my calculated maximum of
$96–$143/unit landed. **You cannot win an open auction against bidders whose freight is shorter
and whose labour is free, and if you do win it, that is the problem, not the achievement.**
Every successful operator in Part 2 sources through *programmes*. Auctions are how you build
the track record that gets you a programme — treat them as tuition, and buy small.

**3. Floor-loaded vs palletised.** Floor-loading means loose hand-stacking. It raises transit
damage, raises unload labour, and is why my base-case salvage rate is 8% rather than 4%.
**Given the choice at the same price, always take the palletised load.**

## Application requirements, common to all B-Stock storefronts

- Valid **state-specific resale certificate** (Michigan Form 3372 with "Resale at Retail"
  checked plus a Michigan Sales Tax Licence number). Approval typically within one business
  day.
- All sales **final, no returns, no warranty** on the lot.
- Shipping is one of: free-to-buyer, fixed, buyer-arranged, or B-Stock "Binding Shipping"
  (real-time freight quote, provider schedules both pickup and delivery appointments).
- **Costco storefronts additionally prohibit resale within 5 miles of a Costco retail store.**
  Check this against your chosen Livonia/Madison Heights site before you buy Costco loads —
  there are Costco warehouses in Livonia, Madison Heights, Commerce Twp and Auburn Hills.

## Ranked recommendation

1. **Build a Whirlpool/Ohio short-haul programme.** This is the highest-value work you can do
   and it costs nothing but persistence. Call regional distributors and Whirlpool's
   Michigan/Ohio outlet channels directly. Every $1,000 of freight you avoid is $1,000 of
   profit that no competitor can bid away from you.
2. **Builder and multifamily overstock.** Uninstalled new appliances from cancelled or
   over-ordered projects. Best condition reliability in the entire table, zero freight,
   negotiable pricing, and almost nobody works this channel systematically. Low volume, but
   the volume you get is the most profitable volume available.
3. **GE Appliances B-Stock**, when the price is right — 360 miles beats 480.
4. **Home Depot / Best Buy B-Stock**, opportunistically and small.
5. **Lowe's B-Stock from Shippensburg PA** — only at or below my maximum bid, which is roughly
   half the current clearing price. In practice this means you will rarely win, and that is
   the correct outcome.
6. **Never** the fixed-price truckload resellers. They bought at auction and added margin.
