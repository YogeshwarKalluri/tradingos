# PART 9 — WAREHOUSE AND FACILITY

## Real SE Michigan industrial rents (verified asking rates)

| Submarket | Asking rent, industrial | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| **Livonia** | **$8.20 – $8.66 /sq ft/yr** | Cheapest verified in the region |
| Detroit (city average) | $9.66 /sq ft/yr | Range $5–$18 depending on quality |
| **Sterling Heights** | **$9.45 – $9.93 /sq ft/yr** | |
| Detroit market range | $5 – $18 /sq ft/yr | 47 warehouses listed, 6,000–1,000,000 sq ft |

These are **NNN asking rates**. Gross them up by roughly **$3.00–$3.50/sq ft** for CAM,
property taxes, insurance and utilities. **All-in occupancy cost used throughout this study:
$12.00/sq ft/yr for space above 5,000 sq ft, $13.50 for 2,500–5,000 sq ft, $16.00 below 2,500
sq ft** (small units carry a size premium).

## Sizing standard

**14 usable sq ft per appliance** — footprint stacked two high, aisles and staging included —
plus **1,800 sq ft per retail showroom.** Below ~80 units/month you do not need a showroom at
all: appointment-only viewing out of the warehouse, selling through Facebook Marketplace. This
is how every bootstrapped operator actually starts and it is worth roughly $2,400/month at the
outset.

## The six sizes modelled

| Size | All-in $/sq ft/yr | **Monthly cost** | Units on hand it supports | Monthly sales volume it fits | Verdict |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **1,000 sq ft** | $16.00 | **$1,333** | ~70 (no showroom) | up to ~45 u/mo | Viable start. No showroom, no dock, appointment only. |
| **3,000 sq ft** | $13.50 | **$3,375** | ~85 with showroom | ~55 u/mo | Matches the profitable owner-operator configuration in Part 2 |
| **5,000 sq ft** | $12.00 | **$5,000** | ~230 with showroom | **~155 u/mo** | **The right size for the base case's steady state** |
| **10,000 sq ft** | $12.00 | **$10,000** | ~585 with showroom | ~390 u/mo | Only justified past 300 u/mo |
| **15,000 sq ft** | $12.00 | **$15,000** | ~940 with showroom | ~625 u/mo | Optimistic scenario only |
| **20,000 sq ft** | $12.00 | **$20,000** | ~1,300 with showroom | ~870 u/mo | At or beyond the Metro Detroit absorption ceiling |

**At the base case's 199-unit/month peak you need roughly 5,300 sq ft and $5,300/month.** That
is the answer. Anything larger is a bet on a volume the market probably will not give you.

## Physical requirements and cost to fit out

| Item | Requirement | One-time cost | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Dock** | One dock-high door **or** a ground-level drive-in with a portable dock leveller | $0–$4,000 | Floor-loaded trucks are hand-unloaded; a leveller and a good ramp matter more than a dock |
| **Forklift** | 3,000–5,000 lb, indoor/electric preferred | $6,000–$14,000 used | Not strictly needed below ~100 units/mo |
| **Pallet jack** | 2 × electric | $1,200–$3,000 | |
| **Appliance dollies** | 4 × with stair-climber and strap system | $1,200 | Cheapest damage-prevention money you will spend |
| **Testing stations** | Dedicated bays for wet test, dry test, gas test | $3,000–$6,000 | See electrical/water/gas below |
| **Electrical** | 120V 20A general; **240V/30A dryer and 240V/50A range circuits**; 3-phase not required | $2,500–$6,000 | You cannot verify an electric range or dryer without them |
| **Gas testing** | Natural-gas drop with shutoff, manometer, leak detection, **licensed installation** | $2,000–$5,000 | Michigan mechanical-code work; use a licensed contractor. Do not improvise gas. |
| **Water** | Hot/cold hookups + floor drain for washers and dishwashers | $2,000–$5,000 | A floor drain is often the single most expensive item on this list in an older building |
| **Ventilation** | Dryer exhaust runs; general exhaust for gas testing | $1,500–$3,500 | |
| **Security** | Alarm, cameras, good locks; appliances are high-value and portable | $1,500–$3,000 + $60/mo | |
| **Showroom fit-out** | Flooring, lighting, signage, POS | $8,000–$25,000 | Skip entirely for the first year |
| **Parking** | Customer parking for 6–10 vehicles; truck turning radius | — | **Check this before signing.** Many cheap Livonia/Detroit industrial units have no customer parking and no room to turn a 53' trailer. |
| **Loading/unloading area** | Space to stage a full 47-unit truck without blocking the aisle | — | Budget 700 sq ft of clear staging |

**Total fit-out, no showroom: $18,000–$40,000.** With showroom: **$26,000–$65,000.**

**This is the number that breaks the $20,000 plan.** You cannot pay for inventory *and*
240V/gas/water testing infrastructure *and* a forklift out of $20,000. Something gives, and
what usually gives is testing — which is exactly how the bear case in Part 5 happens.

## Insurance and carrying costs (Michigan, 2026)

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| General liability | $400–$1,500/yr (as low as $26/mo for basic) |
| Commercial property / contents on inventory | **[ESTIMATE]** $1,200–$3,000/yr at $50K inventory |
| **Workers' compensation** | **Mandatory from the first employee — no small-team exemption in Michigan.** National median $54/mo; rated per $100 of payroll by class code, and warehouse/delivery classes are not cheap. |
| Commercial auto (box truck) | **$1,800–$3,500 per vehicle per year** |

## Recommended configuration

**Months 1–9:** 1,000–1,500 sq ft in **Livonia**, no showroom, appointment-only, Facebook
Marketplace and OfferUp as the storefront. **~$1,300–$2,000/month.** Fit out 240V and water
testing only — defer gas, forklift and showroom.

**Months 10–24:** step to **5,000 sq ft with a 1,000–1,800 sq ft showroom** once you are
consistently above 80 units/month. **~$5,000–$5,300/month.** Add forklift, gas testing, full
bays.

**Beyond:** do not take 10,000+ sq ft unless you have held above 300 units/month for six
months and have the working capital in Part 16 already in hand. In the base case you never
get there, and taking the space early is the most common way this business dies.
